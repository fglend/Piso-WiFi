Defaults & Dashboard

Metering

{% set pausing_on = app_settings.pause_on_disconnect in ('1', 'true', 'yes', 'on') %}
On — time is screen time. A customer's countdown freezes by itself the moment they leave the network and resumes when they come back.
Off — time is elapsed time. The countdown keeps running while the device is away, so a day, week or monthly package expires on schedule instead of lasting for months of occasional use. Shop downtime is never charged.
{% set manual_pause_on = app_settings.allow_manual_pause in ('1', 'true', 'yes', 'on') %}
Shows the Pause my time button on the portal. A customer who taps it stops their countdown and their internet until they tap Resume — this works even when automatic pausing above is off, so someone who knows they are leaving can deliberately stop their own clock first.
Turn this off if a package should always expire on elapsed time with no way for a customer to stretch it.

Danger Zone

{# Listed above Reset revenue because it is the reversible one: it touches no records, so a mistaken click costs nothing. #}

Clear unpaid connected devices

Drops every connected device with no time balance from the dashboard: re-blocks it, clears its stale lease and neighbour entries, and forgets it. No records, balances or history are deleted — a device that is still connected reappears on the next poll, and anyone with time left keeps their access. Paused customers still hold time, so they are never affected.

Reset revenue

Sets today/week/month revenue back to zero by clearing the revenue ledger. Device balances, vouchers and time logs are kept. This cannot be undone.
